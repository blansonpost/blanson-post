Blanson F.C. Beats Victory 4-0

By The Blanson Post

In Blanson's second-ever soccer game in school history, the team secured a decisive 4--0 victory over Victory ECHS.

Goal 1: Javier Arista

Post Game Interview with team captain, Javier Arista

As team captain, how do you feel about Saturday's win against Victory?

"I feel really great because we won four to zero. I hope we get to play like that again in the upcoming games we have. I am really proud of my team."

Blanson is 2-0 in preseason, how do you want the team to build on this performance moving forward?

"We could work on it way better than what we do now."

Goal 2: Miguel "Migi" Aguinaga

Post Game Interview with Miguel "Migi" Aguinaga

How does it feel to score in back-to-back games?

"It feels amazing. I hope to maintain it."

Do you feel any pressure now that you've scored in consecutive games?

"No, I don't feel any pressure. I don't know what pressure is."

Which goal was your favorite--the game versus Avalos or Victory?

"I prefer the goal against the Victory because it was one versus one"

Goal 3: Jonathan Lagos

Goal 4: Mario Flores

Post Game Interview with Coach Guzman-Hernandez

Blanson is 2-0 in preseason, what is the biggest challenge you faced on the field so far?

"I think the biggest challenge so far is the accuracy of our shots. We have missed too many goal opportunities because we hit the poles. Our accuracy is a bit off."

How do you plan to prepare for the next match based on Saturday's dominant performance?

"I plan to prepare my athletes with more conditioning to help our endurance and give them drills that will help their accuracy. My goal is for every single forward, wing and mid, to score at least two goals this season."

How would you describe the team's mentality right now?

"We are confident because we know we have a strong team. We also know that we could be better so we are ready to keep working hard."
