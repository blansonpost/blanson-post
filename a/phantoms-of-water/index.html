Phantoms of Water

By Jason Borrego
Phantoms Of Water written by Jason Borrego
Drip, Drip, Drip...
I lay in my room listening to the sound,
Eyes wide open, yet still bed bound.
Coming from outside my room,
Just across the hall, the sound continues to loom.
I eventually get up and enter the halls,
Listening for what lies beneath the walls.
Some nights, the sound is quiet;
Others, loud like a riot.
Sometimes, I can hear it during the day,
And I'm unable to keep it at bay.
And tonight,
It is anything but light.
I notice that there are other things that send these louder pings.
When days are particularly rough,
And I feel I've had enough,
They keep me up at night.
It never feels right.
I roam the halls, eager for a source, and begin searching on-course.
I've done this before, opening every door, checking every space,
Only to find nothing out of place.
Maybe tonight is unique,
Maybe I'll finally find the leak.
So I search...
And search...and search...
But once again, my hopeful elation, only turns to frustration.
All I can hear is that ringing as it becomes louder in my mind, clinging.
Drip, Drip Drip...
