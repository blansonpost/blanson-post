A few things worth knowing

All three pull from one shared set of articles. Whichever gets picked, adding a story later means writing it once — it does not have to be re-done per design.

The text is copied exactly from what students wrote on the old site, typos and all, so nothing has been reworded on anyone's behalf. Four pieces have no byline anywhere on the old site; those show as “The Blanson Post” until someone can tell us who wrote them.

Once a design is chosen, the other two get deleted and the winner gets the part that actually matters: a login so students can publish their own articles without anyone touching the code.
