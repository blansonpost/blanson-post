Believe
By Josue Reyes
Everyday is a struggle,
A struggle to keep going
When everything seems dark.
You might never want to get up
And keep on living.
Living despite the darkness, however,
Is the strongest thing you could do.
Letting the days go by
While you wallow in negativity,
Is the biggest mistake you could ever make.
While the pessimism in your head
Can feel comforting,
It does not mean that it is self-destructive.
Letting the bad thoughts linger will only lead to regret
You might not think of yourself as something great
And it might be easier as to just give up on the future,
But nothing is out of reach.
Great things are achievable
But you must first believe that you can achieve those things.
The work that you might have to put in might seem daunting,
But it is necessary in order to do what you want to do.
The light at the end of the tunnel is not something made up,
However it only comes to those that truly believe in it.
Your life will not magically become better
If you let it just pass by.
To truly be free from all the negativity,
And all the torment from your brain,
You have to grow your wings and fly.
