There floats the shiny green balloon,
Free as can be, cheery without worry.
Time passes, and the balloon continues to float,
Though there is a subtle change:
It's become smaller, its color now yellow,
Like the yolk of an egg, slightly dimmer.
With time, the balloon continues to float,
Now having shown another change:
It's even smaller, the color now orange,
The dimming flame becomes smaller.
But again, nothing is wrong.
Time jumps again, the balloon is barely afloat,
Its appearance, now undeniable.
Smaller than ever, its color now red,
As if holding onto its last breath.
The balloon starts to deflate,
To the balloon's surprise,
It is resupplied plenty, by the concern of many,
its color, its shape, its form,
It is released, allowed to levitate once again.
Once again free, once again at ease.
floats the shiny green balloon.
